- 2026-07-26: Bourg generator — an organic Normandy town as a new `MapKind = 'bourg'`, `src/sim/bourg/*` (T1-T14; T16 campaign wiring deliberately withheld until art sign-off, so the kind is unreachable from `CHAR_TERRAIN` and every legacy art baseline is untouched). Ten decisions, plan: [../plans/2026-07-26-town-gen-design.md](../plans/2026-07-26-town-gen-design.md). **D1** new kind, fully guarded branch — `'town'` stays byte-identical, because it is pinned by the density test and by every sweep baseline. **D2** the branch runs on derived sub-streams: exactly ONE `rng.int(0x7fffffff)` on the shared stream inside the guard, then `mulberry32(base ^ imul(tag, 0x9e3779b1))` per stage, tags frozen 1-14 and never renumbered. This is the highest-leverage structural choice in the whole effort — without it, retuning stage 8's parcel widths reshuffles stages 9-13 and every screenshot. **D3** `validate`'s 36 A\* calls replaced by three 8-connected flood fills mirroring `path.ts`'s corner rule exactly: semantics-preserving, draws no rng, O(1) in building count instead of linear. Measured 320x240: `'town'` 59.6→5.5 ms, `'open'` 40.9→6.3 ms, i.e. a standalone win for all six existing kinds, and the thing that makes a 65-building map affordable at all. **D4** one `Building` per roof mass, grouped by `groupId` — a terrace of five must read as five roofs. **D5** party walls between neighbours are `EDGE.WALL`, shared spans inside a group `EDGE.NONE`; a `WINDOW` on a party wall is a free LOS hole into next door. **D6** gates are `EDGE.DOOR` in a wall run: `canStep` already passes foot and blocks vehicles, so "infantry-permeable, vehicle-proof" costs zero new sim surface. **D7** free-standing walls carry tops of 1 m (see-over) or 2 m (blocks standing LOS); neither grants cover — `los()` sources cover from `TerrainDef.cover`/`WINDOW`/`coverBehind` only, and low-wall cover would need a new `edgeCover` array. Recorded gap, not an oversight. **D8** no new terrain key — `TERRAIN_KEYS` indices are positional, and the place is carved as `road`, which is exactly what `drawPaving`'s `pavable` test wants. **D9** objectives extended and TAGGED (`MapPlan.objectiveKinds`): a 110-tile-wide town with one central objective leaves its whole edge outside `GARRISON_RADIUS`, so a deterministic greedy coverage fill runs to 12 slots (the doc's 8 leaves buildings uncovered — measured), and tooling reads the tag instead of guessing the index. **D10** rotation-free: curvature comes from topology (jogs, tangent streets, chamfers), never from angle — a diagonal staircase of axis-aligned edges is impassable to `astar`'s no-corner-cutting rule while `los()` sees straight through it. Two constraints are gameplay requirements, not side effects: no straight 3-wide road run inside the envelope exceeds one approach arm (the place widening is held 3 columns clear of each jog — without that it bridged the joint and merged two 45 m arms into one 101 m lane an MG on the place covers end to end), and the rear-garden network must be one foot-connected component spanning the envelope (the flanking layer; a bourg without it is a corridor shoot with nice roofs). **Rejected**: tensor fields for street direction (they exist to produce curvature, which D10 forbids); the polygon straight skeleton for block ridges (500+ lines of event ordering and float-epsilon soup whose output differs under different rounding — a multi-source integer BFS is exact and 2 ms); Delaunay/Fortune Voronoi in the core (jittered-grid nearest-site in one O(9N) integer pass has no cocircular degeneracy and no float predicates); space colonisation for STREETS (dendritic dead-ending is right for farm tracks and wrong for a bourg core, where the network must close); a `cobble` terrain key (buys a move-cost knob at the price of the whole positional tile-index contract); a map version flag (there is no persisted map — the battle is regenerated from `(seed, w, h, kind)` at `bridge.ts:135`, so no migration surface exists). Perf: mean 18.0 ms / max 31.3 ms over 200 seeds, 0/200 first-attempt rejects
- 2026-07-27: Building interiors — procedural rooms and furnishing for every building on every kind, `src/sim/interiors/*` (I1-I12), behind `GenerateOpts.interiors` (default false). Twelve decisions, plan: [../plans/2026-07-26-interiors-design.md](../plans/2026-07-26-interiors-design.md). **D1** terrain is ONE 2-D layer shared by every storey and so are the walls — `GameMap` has no level axis and neither do `terrainAt`/`canStep`/`los()`/`astar`, so the room plan and the furniture plan are authored once and are true on floors 0..n-1; "bedrooms upstairs" is the same shell read differently, and heavy furniture is authored wall-hugging so it reads as dresser downstairs and wardrobe up. **D2** furnishing is a post-pass inside `generateMap`, after every other draw and before `validate`: flag off = zero draws and zero writes, so every pinned fingerprint holds by construction. **D3** four appended terrain keys (`furn_heavy`, `furn_light`, `hearth`, `altar`) — the terrain key IS the sim→render interface for interiors, and `TERRAIN_NAME`'s exhaustive `Record` makes `tsc` demand the tooltip copy. **D4** heavy furniture is IMPASSABLE (`furn_light` passable at moveCost 2.2): impassable is what makes an aisle an aisle — `astar` routes round a pew block, so the central aisle and the counter's offset gap are real chokes rather than a soft cost gradient. **D5** furniture cover is `coverBehind` (0.40 heavy, 0.50 hearth/altar), not on-tile `cover`: nobody stands on a table, `coverBehind` is exactly "fire over the counter", it is directional so flanking past the end of the run defeats it, and it lights up `sim.coverAt` — and therefore AI cover-seeking, the danger model and the UI move dots — with zero sim changes. **D6** no `featureHeight` on any furniture key: the shoot-over branch fires when the eye-line clears it, and any realistic 0.8-1.1 m height would delete the cover on every ground-level shot. Recorded gap: the substrate cannot express "the tower shoots over the pews". **D7** non-sealing is guaranteed twice — an a-priori keep-clear mask (door aprons, window nooks, stairs+neighbours, building centres, circulation spines) and a per-group BFS commit test; rejection consumes no rng, so the stream is identical whether a candidate lands or not. **D8** room ids are not persisted. **D9** per-structure rng sub-streams key on GEOMETRY, never array index — the roof layer's `hash(i, salt)` lesson applied before the mistake; implemented one notch harder than written, on the MASS BBOX rather than `buildings[0]`'s rect, because keying on the group leader would make a church's interior depend on whether the nave or the tower was emitted first. **D10** the pass owns interior edges and clears them first (party walls between different `buildingId`s are never touched), which is also how the church gets its tower door. **D11** every mass of interior area >= 20 gets a second exterior door — on the bourg this turns T8's rear-garden network from a bypass into an entry network; gated on "all existing doors share ONE side" rather than the doc's `doors.length === 1`, because a bourg church can resolve three doors all facing south, and the side-set rule subsumes the count rule exactly. **D12** vehicle exclusion moved from a hard-coded key blacklist in `canStep` and `findVehicleSpot` to a `TerrainDef.noVehicle` flag — semantics-identical for the existing seven keys, and new keys are covered automatically instead of silently letting a vehicle order be dropped inside a building. **Rejected**: a per-level terrain array (a new `GameMap` array plus level-aware `los()`/`astar`/`canStep` — three hot paths rewritten for cosmetic gain); a room-id map array (a second source of truth nothing downstream needs; per-room floor material is served by `Building.role` plus a hearth-proximity test); on-tile `cover` for heavy furniture (dead data — `los()` reads the target tile's `cover` and nobody stands on a table); passable heavy furniture (deletes every lane the layout exists to create); `featureHeight` on furniture (see D6); furnishing BEFORE the dome pass (the domes skip `k === 'floor'`, so furniture tiles would stop being skipped and the domes would lift them); scoring generic terrain cover in `findSpot` (would move formation spots on open ground on every map — a large unrelated behaviour change hiding inside an interiors task); a T7 stair hint for the church group (unnecessary — the tower is its own `Building` at 4 storeys, so `stampStructure` already puts a stair inside the tower mask; the 2-storey nave gets a second one of its own and interiors never moves it). **Measured**: the pass costs 0.60 ms on a 160x120 'open', 1.01 on a 160x120 'town' and 3.45 on a 45-building 320x240 bourg (min-of-reps, WITH the dev-only §4.2 post-pass assertion, which `vite build` strips — 3.99 → 2.91 on the bourg with it stripped); a furnished bourg generation is 24.3 ms mean against the 40 ms budget. Reject-rate delta 0. **Balance**: production opts in at `bridge.ts` and `scenario.ts`, and this is a real fight-length change, intended per §5.1 — the same seed-5 campaign battle resolves in 11,918 ticks unfurnished, 21,038 furnished, and 27,985 once `findSpot` garrisons the window nooks (I10). - 2026-07-27: Baked-art staleness is ONE mechanism (`src/render/versions.ts`, destructible design D9) — the renderer bakes terrain chunks against the edge bytes under them, roof sprites against damage and wall openings, and (once the sun moves, day/night N4/N5) everything with a cast shadow in it against the sun STEP. Rather than a bespoke invalidation per cache, `Versions` answers one question — "is this artifact's art still current" — as a single integer: `bump(key)` for one artifact, `bumpAll()` for every artifact at once in O(1), folded by `at(key)`. The stamp is a monotonic SEQUENCE number, not a per-key counter, because a per-key counter cannot express bumpAll without walking a keyspace the size of the map. Two properties are load-bearing and were chosen against the obvious alternative: (a) an unbumped record answers 0 for every key, so a battle with no breach and no sun step rebakes nothing and every art baseline stays byte-identical (verified: 18 of 20 sweep crops byte-identical old-vs-new; the three that differ — s1-enemy, s10-enemy, s10-guns — differ by MORE between two runs of the same code, the known drawHearthGlow flicker); (b) it EVICTS NOTHING. Deleting the canvas on invalidate is the obvious implementation and it is wrong: stale art is not missing art, it holds the right ground with the wrong wall in it, and deleting forces every dirtied chunk to rebake in the frame it was dirtied — which for a sun step is the entire visible set, i.e. the ~300 ms stall the per-frame bake budget exists to prevent. So `TerrainLayer.chunk()` serves the stale canvas when out of budget and rebakes when there is budget, exactly as `RoofLayer` already did for `dmgVer`. `chunkSpan()` (pure, tested) names every chunk whose 2-tile overscan DRAWS a given edge — an edge on a 16-tile chunk seam is baked into two canvases and invalidating one leaves half the wall floating beside the hole on one tile in sixteen. `EnclosureIndex.refresh()` re-derives masks and runs from the mutated map by the same code path a fresh index uses (the only version that cannot drift) and reports the box the runs MOVED in, which is the whole run and not just the hole, because the rubble coursing is phased along a run; `pockets()` is deliberately not re-run — a garden with a hole in its wall is still a garden, and re-flooding would move the ground dressing under the player. `RoofLayer` gains `wallVer` beside `dmgVer`: the wall band was reused across a damage rebake on the stated grounds that walls are damage-independent (still true) but it is not breach-independent, so the reuse is now conditional; the ground shadow keeps its unconditional reuse, since a hole in a wall does not shorten the throw of the roof above it. Breach art is strict plan projection — two broken stubs in `drawEdge`'s own `#2c241a`/`#4a4034` pair, scattered stone with one cast shadow each, and a lit crown drawn as the same footprint offset UP-sun rather than as a rotated highlight (a rotated highlight gives every stone its own sun). The sim's width byte drives the read so the art cannot disagree with who can cross: BREACH_LIP heaps a ridge across the opening, BREACH_WIDE sweeps the middle clear. `main.ts` drains `takeBreaches()` BESIDE `takeBooms()` and never through it — `addBooms` stamps a scorch and punches a roof hole, and a crushed fence is neither — and the invalidation is unconditional on fog, because a chunk never marked stale is never rebaked and the wall would reappear when the fog lifted. Proven by `scripts/breach.mjs`, which shoots the art read and the sim's own canStep/los answers on the same edge in one row → [architecture.md](architecture.md)
- 2026-07-27: Renderer smoke reads the sim's plume (verticality V12) — `drawSmoke` draws N puffs up the column at `smokeAxisAt(p, z)`, sized from `p.r`, and `sightBetween` re-projects its ray against the same function, so the drawn cloud and the blocking cloud are the same object by construction rather than by two implementations agreeing today. The deleted `smokeMaxTtl` map was not merely redundant, it was WRONG: it seeded a per-entry spawn-ttl from the first `p.ttl` it ever saw, so `elapsed` was 0 — and the puff invisible — for anything the renderer did not watch spawn (a staged scene, an inspected save, a resumed snapshot). Five puffs staged at ages 1/5/15/30/45 drew nothing at all under the old code. `p.age + p.ttl` IS the spawn ttl, so the envelope needs no memory. Per-sample alpha is deliberately NOT normalized by sample count: a taller column has more samples but spreads them over more ground track, so stack depth at a point is already roughly flat — dividing by n as well was tried, screenshotted, and rejected for turning a 60 s screen into a pale smudge. `scripts/lib/stage.mjs` gains `stageSmoke` and `shot.mjs` an `--effect smoke`, because this art had no inspection path at all: sim smoke is frozen under pause, so `burst()`'s millisecond buckets can never see it and AGE is the whole variable
- 2026-07-27: Cast shadows are SWEPT, never translated (day/night follow-up) — a baked cast shadow must start at the caster's base and travel the sun direction as one connected piece, like the pole fingers always did. A silhouette merely translated by `shadowOffset` overlaps its caster at noon and passes every canonical-hour baseline, then detaches into a floating gray copy at dawn/dusk when `lenMul` stretches the offset past the footprint — buildings, enclosure walls, graves, yews, canopies, prop lobes and shop awnings all had it. Three primitives in `light.ts` (`sweptRectPath` 6-point hull, `sweptStrokePath` 8-corner convex hull, `capsulePath`), batched as subpaths and filled ONCE under nonzero winding so overlapping masses never double-darken; hedge strokes sweep by the equivalent stroke-at-half-throw-widened-by-throw trick since a stroked path has no hull. Second rule: `SHADOW_LEN_LEGIBLE = 2.4` clamps `lenMul` for the big baked casters (a game map is not a photograph — solar.ts's 3x rake dragged a two-storey house across half a street; the alpha ramp carries the low-sun story past the clamp), while unit/vehicle/telegraph-pole shadows keep their full deliberate rake. Fixed alphas that ignored `sun().alphaMul` (graves 0.34, gate leaves, shop clutter) now ride it. Bonus: the prop post stamp is 5.2 m with the post centred, ending the known dawn clip at the old 1.4 m stamp edge. Noon baselines: 0.07-0.15% px deltas (corner wedges of the hulls), judged by eye at 0700/1400/2000/0200

- 2026-07-27: Destructible-terrain AI nudges (design D7) — a breach is free for pathing and vision because both read the edge bytes live, but two things had to be pushed. (a) **Forced repath**: a man already walking does not gain the shortcut until something makes him ask again, and an ordinary `move` order never repaths at all, so `breachEdge` QUEUES a repath (through the existing `pendingPaths`/`PATH_BUDGET_PER_TICK` machinery, to the soldier's own current destination) for `move`/`assault` men within 25 m.
