- 2026-07-27: Baked-art staleness is ONE mechanism (`src/render/versions.ts`, destructible design D9) — the renderer bakes terrain chunks against the edge bytes under them, roof sprites against damage and wall openings, and (once the sun moves, day/night N4/N5) everything with a cast shadow in it against the sun STEP. Rather than a bespoke invalidation per cache, `Versions` answers one question — "is this artifact's art still current" — as a single integer: `bump(key)` for one artifact, `bumpAll()` for every artifact at once in O(1), folded by `at(key)`. The stamp is a monotonic SEQUENCE number, not a per-key counter, because a per-key counter cannot express bumpAll without walking a keyspace the size of the map. Two properties are load-bearing and were chosen against the obvious alternative: (a) an unbumped record answers 0 for every key, so a battle with no breach and no sun step rebakes nothing and every art baseline stays byte-identical (verified: 18 of 20 sweep crops byte-identical old-vs-new; the three that differ — s1-enemy, s10-enemy, s10-guns — differ by MORE between two runs of the same code, the known drawHearthGlow flicker); (b) it EVICTS NOTHING. Deleting the canvas on invalidate is the obvious implementation and it is wrong: stale art is not missing art, it holds the right ground with the wrong wall in it, and deleting forces every dirtied chunk to rebake in the frame it was dirtied — which for a sun step is the entire visible set, i.e. the ~300 ms stall the per-frame bake budget exists to prevent. So `TerrainLayer.chunk()` serves the stale canvas when out of budget and rebakes when there is budget, exactly as `RoofLayer` already did for `dmgVer`. `chunkSpan()` (pure, tested) names every chunk whose 2-tile overscan DRAWS a given edge — an edge on a 16-tile chunk seam is baked into two canvases and invalidating one leaves half the wall floating beside the hole on one tile in sixteen. `EnclosureIndex.refresh()` re-derives masks and runs from the mutated map by the same code path a fresh index uses (the only version that cannot drift) and reports the box the runs MOVED in, which is the whole run and not just the hole, because the rubble coursing is phased along a run; `pockets()` is deliberately not re-run — a garden with a hole in its wall is still a garden, and re-flooding would move the ground dressing under the player. `RoofLayer` gains `wallVer` beside `dmgVer`: the wall band was reused across a damage rebake on the stated grounds that walls are damage-independent (still true) but it is not breach-independent, so the reuse is now conditional; the ground shadow keeps its unconditional reuse, since a hole in a wall does not shorten the throw of the roof above it.
