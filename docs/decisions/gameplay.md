# Gameplay & AI Decisions

### [APPROVED] Order execution (interpret + refuse) and tactical AI commander (honest vision)
**Date:** 2026-07-22
**Context:** The approved 7-order vocabulary had no behavioral design; the maneuver-AI pillar had no architecture. Specs: [order-execution](../specs/2026-07-22-order-execution.md), [ai-commander](../specs/2026-07-22-ai-commander.md).
**Decision:**
1. Orders are intent, not waypoints: steady troops interpret (auto-bounding under fire, cover micro, emergent formations) and can refuse suicidal orders before psychology breaks — comply / comply-cautious / balk ladder with mandatory UI reason chips (legibility pillar). Counterplay: suppress, smoke, sneak, leader presence.
2. Mount/dismount, deploy/pack, hitch/unhitch, aid/drag, pickup are context actions, not vocabulary; exposed ones run the same compliance check.
3. One shared danger/threat model, three consumers: soldier refusal, team firing-position choice, AI commander plan scoring. Tuned once.
4. AI commander plays through the player's order API only (no hidden micro, no cheats); honest vision + pre-battle briefing; decaying threat map from unit contacts. Its troops balk too — the refusal model forces doctrinal play (suppress before assault) without doctrine scripts. Two layers: commander (belief state, plan, replan-on-trigger) + task groups (base of fire / maneuver / reserve / screen).
5. Difficulty tunes commander skill (replan latency, coordination, spending), never information. Harness gains doctrine-health metrics (suppression-before-assault %, AI balk rate, honesty audit).
**Alternatives considered:** interpret-without-refusal (loses the CC signature); literal waypoint execution (wastes the per-soldier sim); omniscient commander (kills recon/ambush play and poisons resolver calibration); tunable-honesty dial (deferred: honest + optional dial later if difficulty demands).

### [APPROVED] Unified cover model: directional lip cover, cover/concealment split, deterministic sneak spotting
**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Context:** Cover grew piecemeal across M1–M5 — cover math in four places, omnidirectional (tile you stand in; windows the only directional case), concealment conflated with cover (opacity only gated binary LOS), and the sneak order had no detection layer (sneaking units always seen).
**Decision:**
1. One sight pipeline, three layers: `los()` computes directional cover = max(target-tile cover, lip cover, window 0.5); `sightBetween` adds smoke; `shotPreview` adds stance (pinned/downed +0.3, never reducing cover). All data-driven from `TerrainDef`.
2. **Lip cover** (`TerrainDef.coverBehind`, hedge 0.5): a shot crossing the terrain within 2m of the target grants that cover — the window rule generalized. Hugging a hedgerow bank (0.5) beats standing in the bushes (0.35) and only holds against shots crossing the hedge; flanking defeats it. Hedgerows stay 1-tile terrain, not edges.
3. **Concealment split**: `LosResult.conceal` = accumulated opacity + smoke (0..1; ≥1 still blocks — two-hedge rule unchanged). Cover stops bullets; concealment stops eyes.
4. **Spotting rule** (completes sneak, deterministic, no RNG): `sightToSoldier` = sightBetween + "sneaking target with conceal ≥ 0.5 is unseen beyond 20m". Every soldier-target sense routes through it (acquire, live fire, ambush trigger, `visibleEnemies` honest vision) so the danger model and AI commander inherit stealth; sight to a map point stays `sightBetween`. Previews surface it as reason `concealed` ("target concealed"), never a bare no-LOS.
5. **Positional summary** `sim.coverAt(x,y)` = max(tile cover, adjacent coverBehind); `inCover` = coverAt > 0.2 or interior floor — still the one definition shared by AI cover-seeking, danger scoring, and move-preview dots, now bocage-bank-aware.
**Alternatives considered:** converting hedgerows to edges (rejected: 1-tile-terrain is a logged mapgen decision; edges are for interiors); probabilistic detection rolls (rejected: RNG in vision breaks determinism and order-preview legibility); per-soldier stance system (deferred: pinned/downed hug-ground already covers the payoff at current scope).

### [APPROVED] Fog of war: sim-owned per-side spotting, last-known contacts, ambush concealment
**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Context:** The player was omniscient (renderer drew everything; tooltips probed anything; HUD printed the exact enemy headcount) while the red AI already played under honest vision. FoW makes the player's intel as honest as the AI's and turns the concealment layer into visible gameplay.
**Decision:**
1. The sim owns "what a side knows": `updateSpotting()` sweeps every 15 ticks (0.5 s) per side — a living un-downed friendly or alive vehicle within `SPOT_RANGE` 200 m with `sightToSoldier` LOS spots enemy soldiers (concealment-aware); `sightBetween` spots vehicles. Deterministic, no RNG, never read back by sim behavior. Two intel layers stay distinct: `visibleEnemies` = engagement/danger semantics (weapon-range), `spotted` = knowledge semantics (SPOT_RANGE); both look through `sightToSoldier`.
2. `spotted` maps carry the last-seen position; losing LOS converts it to a fading last-known contact (45 s TTL, dashed "?" marker). Statics — corpses, wrecks, emplacements — are permanent once seen (`seenStatic`), and finding one resolves its ghost.
3. **Ambush concealment**: the sneak spotting rule extends to untriggered `ambush` orders (stationary, holding fire, conceal ≥ 0.5, > 20 m). The trigger converts to defend, so the first volley reveals the position — the hidden-first-volley moment. This also removes ambushers from the enemy's danger model until they fire (honest: you can't balk at what you can't see).
4. UI consumes exactly three helpers — `soldierVisibleTo` / `vehicleVisibleTo` / `emplacementVisibleTo` — in the renderer, `pickAt`, and the HUD (GE count becomes "~spotted"); enemy unit-event pings gate on `sideSeesPoint`. `fowDisabled` (?fow=0 / `__game.setFow(false)`) is read only by the helpers, never by `step()`. Tracers/muzzle flashes render unfiltered — fire revealing itself IS the intel.
**Alternatives considered:** muzzle-flash "fire contacts" (rejected as structurally redundant: LOS is symmetric, so any shooter in weapon range is spotted at the next 0.5 s sweep — the renderer's existing muzzle-flash effect already gives the moment); render-side FoW (rejected: UI re-deriving vision violates the one-definition rule); tile shroud/explored-map (rejected: company-scale daytime maps — the ground is known, the enemy isn't); RNG detection rolls (rejected: determinism + legibility).

### [APPROVED] Melee close combat: proximity lock, strike cycles through the wound model
**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Context:** The sim had ranged combat only — assaults that closed to arm's length ended as point-blank rifle duels, and there was no cost to walking through an occupied room or over a broken defender.
**Decision:**
1. **Trigger — proximity, no new order.** An eligible soldier (alive, on foot, not downed/pinned/broken) within 2.0m of an enemy along a walkable tile chain (`canStep`: doors pass, walls and windows don't) locks both into melee automatically. Assaults, room clearing, hedge crossings, and overruns produce contact naturally; the victim of an engaged attacker fights back even if pinned. N-vs-1 allowed — extra attackers pile onto the same victim, who strikes back at whoever engaged him first.
2. **Lock.** Melee overrides tasks, ranged targeting, and movement; the only motion is closing to contact (0.9m) via wall-aware straight steps. Disengage only by outcome: opponent dead, downed, or gone past 2.5m. Breaking (morale < 0.25) is the one way out — a broken man drops the lock and flees, and a pursuer keeps striking while he's in strike reach (routs at contact get run down); once the runner opens the gap past strike range the pursuit breaks off and the rifle takes over (equal speeds would otherwise freeze the chase into a futile jog).
3. **Resolution — tick-based strike cycles, standard models.** `meleePhase` advances at a jittered tempo (~1.4s per strike, 0.5–0.9s first windup); a strike lands at phase wrap. Hit chance = weapon-class base × `aimFactor` (the one aim formula — pain, suppression, nerves, stamina, veterancy degrade melee exactly like ranged fire). Hits resolve through `wound()` (part roll, pain, bleed, morale shock, fatal check) — no parallel HP system. Misses suppress the target (+0.1); engaging spikes both +0.15; melee drains stamina (0.03/s).
4. **SMG position.** An SMG with rounds left fights melee by shooting from the hip: 0.7 base vs the bayonet's 0.55, weapon damage, consumes a round, emits a real ShotEvent (muzzle flash). SMGs dominate CQC by firing, not clubbing — their tradeoff is ammo. Dry SMGs club at 0.45; MG/AT/mortar hands are clumsy (0.35, [20,50]).
5. **Legibility.** New `MeleeEvent` queue (hit/kill fleck, miss swish, blood decal) mirrors ShotEvent's consume shape; `meleePhase`/`meleeId` are sim state, so the 4-frame melee pose (guard/windup/thrust/recover, fixed bayonet on rifles) freezes on pause like walk. Melee state is in `snapshot()` for determinism tests.
**Alternatives considered:** a charge order (rejected: order-vocabulary change for behavior adjacency already yields); strict 1-1 pairing (rejected: swarming attackers would idle); opposed-roll resolution (rejected: single roll per swing keeps aimFactor the shared, legible skill formula). **Open edges:** `pickFirer` can still serve an emplacement while its crewman is bayonet-fighting. (Broken men caught at melee contact now surrender — see the Surrender entry.)

### [APPROVED] Melee × FoW seam: contact is a sense
**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Context:** Melee landed in a parallel session, before FoW. The lock ignored concealment (smoke stopped rifles but not bayonet locks) with no intel consequence, and MeleeEvent FX/decals drew at fog-hidden positions.
**Decision:** Melee is a **physical-contact sense**. The lock keeps ignoring smoke and concealment — at 2m you bump into a man, and a smoke-covered assault is supposed to end at bayonet point. In exchange, contact is honest intel: `enterMelee` force-spots both fighters to both sides instantly (the lock pose must never point at an unseen man), the spotting sweep's `meleeContact` override keeps a locked fighter spotted while the fight holds (smoke can't ghost him mid-fight), and a melee kill adds the corpse to the killer's `seenStatic` (killed at arm's length — found, not a ghost). A sneaking/ambushing man bumped into melee is revealed, but his team's order is untouched — one compromised man doesn't blow the ambush; when the fight ends he can melt back, leaving the usual ghost. `MeleeEvent` carries `attackerId`/`victimId`; the renderer drops fleck + blood smear unless either fighter passes `soldierVisibleTo`. Stated plainly: this gate is a no-op today by structural guarantee — melee only pairs opposite sides and one of two sides is always the player's, so a fighter the player can see is always present. It stays as cheap insurance for spectator/multi-side futures; the pairing invariant is pinned by a test in melee.test.ts so the gate becoming load-bearing cannot happen silently.
**Alternatives considered:** smoke blocks the melee lock (rejected: two men 2m apart ignoring each other is absurd, and it would neuter smoke-covered assaults); melee reveal converts the whole team's ambush order (rejected: punishes the squad for one man's bad luck; defend-conversion stays fire-triggered only).

### [APPROVED] Assault order: the charge that makes melee a weapon
**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Context:** Melee existed only as a consequence (proximity lock) — nothing in the game deliberately closed to 2m, so close combat couldn't be used. The melee log's "no charge order" rejection was about *triggering*; assault is about *closing*.
**Decision:**
1. **8th order kind `assault`** — take the position at close quarters. Compliance runs on `dangerScore × 1.3` (ASSAULT_DANGER_MULT): the scariest ask in the vocabulary. Comply = charge; cautious = downgrade to `move` ("unwilling to charge — advancing instead", mirroring fast→move); balk = CHARGE_BALK_REASON. Checked once at issuance — a charge that steps off is committed (no bounding, no per-leg re-check); pinned/broken men drop out via the psych model.
2. **Execution.** Initial path to the point; each soldier on a 0.5s cadence (ASSAULT_REPATH_TICKS) redirects onto the nearest *visible* defender within ASSAULT_RADIUS (20m) of the point — the closing loop that feeds the existing 2m melee lock. Marching fire: assault is deliberately absent from the hold-fire gate (men shoot on the move at the standard 0.5 moving penalty). Speed ×1.4, stamina drain 0.015/s — between move and sprint.
3. **Completion.** When every eligible member is pathless, out of melee, and no member sees a defender within the radius *that he could still path to*: order → idle. Position taken; unseen lurkers are normal combat's problem, and so is a visible-but-unreachable holdout (sealed room behind a window, far river bank) — he's a fire problem, not an assault problem, and must not hang the order (review fix 16ff269; repath likewise falls back past unreachable men, and contact only counts if meleeReachable).
4. **Doctrine falls out free:** with defenders in clear LOS of the approach, all 8 danger samples read covered (≥1.2) — a naked charge across open ground balks *by construction*. Assaults comply along concealed approaches (bocage, buildings) or under smoke: the suppress/smoke-then-charge loop emerges from the existing danger model with zero special-case code (verified in tests: same charge balks naked, complies through smoke).
**Alternatives considered:** extending `fast` to auto-close when the destination is enemy-held (rejected: implicit intent, and refusal reasons couldn't name the charge); per-leg compliance re-checks mid-charge (rejected: a committed charge is the point — the drama is whether they step off, not a bounding crawl).
**AI adoption (2026-07-23, follow-up):** the deferred balance pass landed. `RedCommander.runAttack` swaps `fast`→`assault` for a maneuver team once suppression is established, threat confidence at the post is live (≥ CLEAR_CONF), and the team centroid is within `ASSAULT_RANGE` (30m). Same `orderTeam` API the player uses, so the compliance ladder still gates it per-team (a team that shouldn't charge downgrades to move) — `suppressionEstablished` is only the attempt-gate. An executing charge is never reissued (would re-roll compliance and restart the run-in). The reserve-commit loss check runs *before* that guard, so a team bled out mid-charge still triggers reinforcement (wrap-up review fix, regression-tested). **Emergent (verified by probe):** the charge only fires against defenders who *survive the approach and stay spotted* — dug into cover, foot-reachable within 30m. Against open-ground defenders the maneuver team's own fire clears the post before it closes, so `cleared` goes true and it walks in on `fast` — no wasted charge. Whether real campaign maps produce the dug-in case often enough is a playtest/balance question, unmeasured.

### [APPROVED] AI smoke employment: screening the objective before the charge
**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Context:** With assault adoption landed (the follow-up note on the assault-order entry above), the red commander could charge a *dug-in* post — but it still owned mortar smoke it never used, so a defended post in the open (defenders with LOS over the approach) stalled: the naked charge balks *by construction* (assault-order §4), and `cleared` only goes true once the maneuver team's own fire kills the post. The counter the player runs — smoke the objective, then charge the blinded final leg — was the missing half. This adds it, through the same public order API (`orderSmoke`), refusal ladder intact.
**Decision:**
1. **Screen the objective, not the approach.** As the maneuver team enters `SMOKE_RANGE` (45m, a touch beyond charge range) with a live post (suppressed, threat conf ≥ `CLEAR_CONF`), `maybeSmokeObjective` tasks a `readyMortar` — any deployed red mortar not mid-mission with shells its crew can feed, scanned side-wide like the barrage, independent of task-group assignment — via `orderSmoke` onto the post itself. Smoke **on the objective** blinds the position; a thin mid-approach screen wouldn't (the side-wide `visibleEnemies` samples past it stay covered). One mission at a time (`smokeRoundsLeft > 0` is left to finish); a fresh mission is laid on re-eval while the team is still approaching or has balked — the executing-charge guard skips the trigger during the committed run-in, but the initial 4-round screen (each puff 60s ttl) outlasts that seconds-long charge, so it never needs refreshing mid-run.
2. **The refusal ladder is the timer, not a script.** Smoke and the assault issue in the same eval; the first charges balk (screen not yet landed — a naked charge onto the covered post), and only once the smoke drops `dangerScore` at the post does the charge commit (phase `executing`). No "wait for screen" state — compliance *is* the wait, and balks standing is the point (doctrine emerges from the troops' refusal, not a doctrine script). Doctrine-health metric added (`tests/doctrine.test.ts` "smoke before assault"): the committed charge steps off only after a smoke puff sits on the objective; mutation-verified (disable the smoke trigger → the charge balks forever, test fails). Browser-verified: the red AI charges the smoked post into melee.
**Alternatives considered:** rifle-team smoke grenades instead of mortars (rejected: ≤30m throw is usually shorter than the approach, and 2/battle; mortars give sustained side-wide coverage — spec's steer); an explicit "screen up?" gate before ordering the assault (rejected: adds state for what the compliance ladder already does — balk-until-blind is the wait); smoking mid-approach to cover the movement (rejected: only smoke on the objective blinds the defenders).

### [APPROVED] WeaponDef.class: one weapon-taxonomy field, two hand-maintained sets retired
**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Context:** Two independent sets encoded the same SMG/MG taxonomy — `MELEE_SMG` (`src/sim/sim.ts`, the melee hip-shot rule) and `SMG_CLASS`/`MG_CLASS` (`src/render/dudes.ts` `gunClass`, the silhouette bucket) — each a literal `Set(['thompson', 'mp40'])` maintained by hand. Two SMGs exist today; a third would have to be added to both, in two files, or silently render/fight wrong.
**Decision:** Add a required `class: 'rifle' | 'smg' | 'mg'` to every `WeaponDef` (`src/sim/data.ts`) — the silhouette + melee-handling bucket, single source of truth. `MELEE_SMG.has(w)` → `WEAPONS[w].class === 'smg'`; `gunClass(w)` → `WEAPONS[w].class`; both sets deleted. Behavior-preserving by construction: only thompson/mp40 are `smg`, only m1919/mg42 are `mg`, everything else `rifle` — exactly the prior membership (full suite green, including melee.test.ts's thompson hip-shot). A third SMG now needs one `class: 'smg'` and both consumers pick it up. Kept to the three buckets the consumers actually read (YAGNI — no `at`/`mortar` classes nothing distinguishes yet; AT/mortar weapons render rifle-length as before).
**Alternatives considered:** a finer taxonomy (`at`, `mortar`) up front (rejected: no consumer reads them as distinct from `rifle` today — speculative generality); leaving the marker optional and defaulting absent to `rifle` (rejected: a required field on the `satisfies` literal makes a new weapon's class a compile-time obligation, not an easy omission).

### [APPROVED] Grenades: the assault's room-clearing opener, thrown before the bayonet
**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Context:** The assault loop closed straight to bayonets with no way to soften a held room first; the TO&E grenadier was a "10 extra clips, no grenades this milestone" stand-in (data.ts). Melee's lock fires at 2m — too late for an opener — but the assault cadence already closes men through throw range: the natural hook.
**Decision:**
1. **Trigger — automatic during the assault, no new order.** On the assault per-soldier cadence (ASSAULT_REPATH_TICKS), an assaulter with grenades left, off throw cooldown, with LOS to a visible defender within GRENADE_RANGE (12m) whose ground is clustered (≥2 enemies within frag radius) or enclosed, throws one grenade at the defender's position. A lone man in the open is a fire problem, not a grenade problem.
2. **Carrier + budget.** The grenadier TO&E slot carries 2 grenades, replacing the 10-extra-clips stand-in; the count is soldier-scoped (smokeLeft's budget-counter shape, one level down). Everyone else carries 0 — test fixtures stay neutral by default.
3. **Flight + burst.** A thrown grenade rides a pendingGrenades list ({x, y, fuse}, decayed like smoke ttl) and bursts after ~1s (30 ticks) as explode({suppRadius: 6, fragRadius: 3, frags: 2, dmg: [20, 55]}) at a scattered point. Scatter draws angle then dist — one draw each, the mortar convention. Small radii are deliberate: the closing thrower takes edge suppression as the "don't throw too close" signal. explode() is untouched and friendly fire stays honest.
4. **Determinism.** Scatter draws fire exactly once per thrown grenade, in tick/soldier-id order, ahead of the enterMelee tempo draw; nothing keys off render-side state. grenadesLeft and pendingGrenades join snapshot() for the determinism tests.
5. **Legibility.** The burst reuses pendingBooms (audio + shake already wired) — no new event. AI needs no change: the throw lives in the assault path, so both sides grenade.
**Alternatives considered:** a throwable item with a dedicated order (rejected: order-vocabulary change for behavior the assault loop already yields — the melee "no charge order" rejection, one level up); throwing AT the 2m lock inside considerMelee (rejected: too late to soften, and the thrower eats his own point-blank blast — explode() has no friendly-fire check); excluding the thrower from his blast (rejected: explode() surgery for a fiction the small radii already tell); grenades for every rifleman (rejected: unbounded softening — the grenadier budget keeps it a tool, not a barrage). **Open edges:** throw-arc FX (boom-only today); no cooking or pickup.

### [APPROVED] Surrender: broken men at melee contact are captured, not run down
**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Context:** Melee's open edge — broken men caught in melee died rather than surrender. A man who broke in a lock dropped it and fled, and the pursuit rule ran him down. Genre research (Close Combat's surrender control; the unit spec's "dry soldiers fall back to pistol/melee/surrender") expects broken men to give up when overwhelmed.
**Decision:**
1. **Status — a captured flag, not a fifth Mental.** `captured: boolean` on Soldier: alive, out of the fight, untargetable — excluded from targeting and considerMelee exactly like downed. Mental stays the 4-state morale readout; capture is a state, not a mood.
2. **Trigger — overwhelmed, not merely caught.** A broken man within MELEE_ENGAGE (2m) of ≥2 enemies surrenders. A single pursuer still chases and runs him down — the rout-at-contact drama survives for isolated chases; only surrounded routs convert. The check runs at the two broken-contact points — considerMelee (attacker locks an already-broken man) and the break-mid-fight moment in the psych update — counting surrounding enemies BEFORE the flee block clears meleeId: a broken man clears his own meleeId first, so the check keys off the attacker's lock, never the victim's.
3. **Effect.** Captured men drop tasks and locks, stay broken, leave targeting/eligibility, and count as out-of-fighting-strength (like downed) for victory — NOT KIA. No corpse, no kill credit.
4. **FoW + legibility.** The surrender tick force-spots captor and prisoner (contact is a sense — enterMelee's rule), so the hands-up is never fog-hidden. UnitEvent kind 'surrendered' flows through pings/audio/FOW like 'broken'; the renderer draws a hands-up pose. captured joins snapshot().
**Alternatives considered:** a fifth Mental state (rejected: every mental switch in the sim pays for what is really an out-of-combat flag); always-at-contact surrender (rejected: a lone pursuer bayoneting a fleeing man is the melee system's signature drama — over-producing prisoners kills it); a no-escape-path trigger (rejected: pathing queries in the melee hot path for what a ≥2-attacker count already reads as); prisoners counted as KIA (rejected: alive-but-taken is the whole point).
